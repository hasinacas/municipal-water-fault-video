# Video Distribution & Promotion Guide

## OVERVIEW

This document provides comprehensive guidance on how to distribute, promote, and maximize the reach of the municipal water fault reporting video across multiple platforms. It includes platform-specific optimization, promotion strategies, and expected outcomes.

---

## PRE-DISTRIBUTION CHECKLIST

Before uploading to any platform, ensure:

- [ ] Final video exported in all required formats
- [ ] Captions/subtitles are accurate and timed correctly
- [ ] Audio is balanced (voiceover -3dB, music -18dB)
- [ ] Color grading is consistent across all scenes
- [ ] No visual artifacts or compression issues
- [ ] All branding/logo elements are in place (if applicable)
- [ ] Legal review completed (if required by municipality)
- [ ] Stakeholder sign-off received
- [ ] All contact information and links are current
- [ ] Social media accounts are verified and active

## WHATSAPP & WHATSAPP BUSINESS

### WhatsApp Distribution Strategy

#### Video Format for WhatsApp
- **Resolution**: 1280x720 (16:9 ratio)
- **File Size**: Keep under 100MB (heavily compressed)
- **Duration**: 3-4 minutes
- **Format**: H.264 MP4
- **Bitrate**: 2-3 Mbps (optimized for mobile data)

#### WhatsApp Broadcast/Channel Distribution
1. **Save Video to WhatsApp Media Library**
   - Save MP4 file to phone storage
   - Open WhatsApp > Share to ensure it uploads correctly

2. **Distribution Methods**

   **Option A: WhatsApp Business API**
   - Set up WhatsApp Business account with municipality
   - Create Broadcast List of subscriber numbers
   - Share video directly with all subscribers
   - Add caption: See templates below

   **Option B: WhatsApp Community Feature** (if available)
   - Create "Water Fault Reporting" community
   - Invite residents to join
   - Post video with caption
   - Enable community links for easy sharing

   **Option C: Manual Sharing**
   - Share video link (YouTube or cloud storage) via WhatsApp groups
   - Use municipal WhatsApp group
   - Include caption with each share

#### WhatsApp Caption Template
```
📞 HOW TO REPORT A WATER FAULT

Did you report a water fault? Keep your reference number!

Watch this video to learn:
✓ Why your reference number is CRITICAL
✓ What the 48-hour response means
✓ How to follow up correctly
✓ DO NOT create duplicate complaints

🎬 Watch now: [YouTube Link or Cloud Link]

REMEMBER:
• 48 hours = attendance, NOT completion
• Keep your original reference number
• Use it to follow up
• Don't report the same fault twice

Questions? Call: [Number] | Email: [Email]

Share with family & friends! 👇
```

#### Video Link for WhatsApp
- **Best Option**: YouTube link (shorter, recognizable, trackable)
- **Alternative**: Cloud storage link (Google Drive, Dropbox)
- **Not Recommended**: Direct MP4 file (too large, poor compatibility)

### Timing & Frequency
- **Launch Date**: Coordinate with municipality media release
- **Initial Send**: Send to all subscribers on launch day
- **Reminder**: Resend 1 week after launch
- **Ongoing**: Include in weekly or monthly citizen updates
- **Peak Times**: Tuesday-Thursday, 10am-2pm (highest engagement)

## SUCCESS FACTORS

### Keys to Maximizing Reach

1. **Consistency**: Post regularly on all platforms
2. **Engagement**: Respond to comments and questions quickly
3. **Cross-Platform**: Link between YouTube, social media, email
4. **Partnerships**: Leverage existing networks for distribution
5. **Timing**: Post when residents are most active (peak hours)
6. **Relevance**: Share when water issues are in the news
7. **Accessibility**: Ensure video is accessible (captions, low-data version)
8. **Call-to-Action**: Always include clear next steps
9. **Monitoring**: Track metrics and adjust strategy accordingly
10. **Feedback**: Collect resident feedback and iterate

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

- ❌ Uploading to one platform only
- ❌ No follow-up promotion after week 1
- ❌ Ignoring comments and feedback
- ❌ No tracking of results/metrics
- ❌ Using outdated contact information
- ❌ Inconsistent messaging across platforms
- ❌ No effort to engage partners/influencers
- ❌ Assuming video will go viral without promotion
- ❌ Not optimizing for mobile viewing
- ❌ Forgetting to update links/descriptions when information changes
