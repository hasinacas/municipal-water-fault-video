# Municipal Water Fault Video - Production Timeline

## Total Project Duration

**FAST-TRACK (Outsourced): 2-4 weeks**
**IN-HOUSE (Full Production): 6-12 weeks**
**MINIMAL BUDGET (DIY Animation): 8-16 weeks**

---

## PHASE 1: PRE-PRODUCTION (1-2 weeks)

### Week 1
- **Script finalization & approval**: 2-3 days
  - Review and refine voiceover script
  - Align messaging with municipal communications team
  - Estimate: 8-16 hours work

- **Storyboard creation**: 3-4 days
  - Create detailed visual storyboards for all 8 scenes
  - Design mood/color palette
  - Estimate: 16-24 hours work

- **Character design & style guide**: 2-3 days
  - Design 4-5 diverse South African resident characters
  - Create animation style guide (flat design, colors, typography)
  - Estimate: 12-20 hours work

- **Voice talent booking**: 1-2 days
  - Source voiceover artist(s) - South African accent preferred
  - Estimate: 4-8 hours work

### Week 2 (Optional)
- **Voiceover recording & editing**: 2-3 days
  - Record professional voiceover
  - Edit audio for timing
  - Estimate: 8-12 hours work

---

## PHASE 2: PRODUCTION (3-8 weeks)

### Asset Creation (2-3 weeks)

  - Create reusable animation components

  - Water fault visual elements

- **Scene 2 (48-Hour Confusion)**: 2-3 days
  - Clock/calendar animation, character reactions
  - Text animations
  - Estimate: 10-14 hours

- **Scene 3 (Common Mistake)**: 3-4 days
  - Multiple reference numbers appearing
  - Piling visual effect, warning symbol
  - Estimate: 12-18 hours

- **Scene 4 (Timeline)**: 2-3 days
  - Visual timeline animations
  - Reference number transitions
  - Estimate: 8-12 hours

- **Scene 5 (Correct Way)**: 3-4 days
  - Character behavior change
  - Phone screen interaction
  - Estimate: 12-16 hours

- **Scene 6 (48 Hours Passed)**: 2-3 days
  - Escalation flow animation
  - Timeline progression
  - Estimate: 8-12 hours

- **Scene 7 (Recurring Fault)**: 2-3 days
  - Repair sequence, problem recurrence
  - Character emotional arc
  - Estimate: 8-12 hours

- **Scene 8 (Paper Trail)**: 2-3 days
  - Timeline development with emphasis
  - Reference number highlight effects
  - Estimate: 8-12 hours

- **Final Scene (Call to Action)**: 2-3 days
  - Multiple residents display
  - Text reveal animations
  - Professional ending
  - Estimate: 8-12 hours

- **Music & sound design**: 2-3 days
  - Select/license background music (uplifting, professional)
  - Add subtle sound effects (water sounds, notification pings, etc.)
  - Audio mixing and balancing
  - Estimate: 8-12 hours work

- **Color grading & visual polish**: 2-3 days
  - Ensure consistent color scheme throughout
  - Add any final visual enhancements
  - Optimize for different platforms
  - Estimate: 8-12 hours work

- **Video composition & sequencing**: 2-3 days
  - Synchronize animation with voiceover
  - Add text timing
  - Ensure smooth transitions
  - Estimate: 10-16 hours work

- **Quality assurance & revisions**: 2-4 days
  - Internal review
  - Stakeholder feedback incorporation
  - Bug fixes and adjustments
  - Estimate: 8-16 hours work

---

## PHASE 4: EXPORT & DELIVERY (2-3 days)

- **Master file export**: 1 day
  - High-quality master (1080p/4K)
  - Estimate: 2-4 hours work

- **Platform optimization**: 1-2 days
  - WhatsApp optimized version (compressed, auto-play friendly)
  - YouTube version (full quality, optimized description)
  - Instagram/TikTok versions (vertical/square formats)
  - Estimate: 4-8 hours work

- **Captions & subtitles**: 1-2 days
  - Add English captions
  - Optional: Translate to Afrikaans, Zulu, Xhosa
  - Estimate: 4-8 hours work

## TIMELINE BREAKDOWN BY PRODUCTION METHOD

### Method 1: OUTSOURCE TO ANIMATION STUDIO (2-4 weeks)
**Cost**: R15,000 - R35,000 (varies by studio)
- Week 1: Pre-production & script approval
- Week 2-3: Studio produces animation
- Week 4: Revisions & delivery

**Pros**: Fast, professional, handles all aspects
**Cons**: Higher cost, less control, studio availability

---

### Method 2: HIRE FREELANCE ANIMATOR + IN-HOUSE COORDINATION (4-8 weeks)
**Cost**: R8,000 - R18,000
- Weeks 1-2: You create storyboards/script, hire animator
- Weeks 2-6: Animator produces video
- Weeks 6-7: You manage revisions
- Week 8: Final delivery

**Pros**: More affordable, good quality, flexible revisions
**Cons**: Longer timeline, coordination required, quality varies

---

### Method 3: USE ANIMATION SOFTWARE + DIY (8-16 weeks)
**Cost**: Software (Canva Pro R60/month, Animaker R199/month, or Adobe Animate CC R850/month)
- Weeks 1-2: Learn software, create assets
- Weeks 3-10: Animate all scenes (steep learning curve)
- Weeks 11-12: Polish and export
- Weeks 13-14: Captions and optimization

**Pros**: Lowest cost, full control, reusable templates
**Cons**: Time-intensive, requires learning, may need revisions

---

## RECOMMENDED APPROACH FOR SOUTH AFRICAN MUNICIPALITY

**Option: Hybrid Freelance Model (6-8 weeks, R10,000-R20,000)**

1. **Week 1**: Internal script finalization + storyboard creation
2. **Week 2**: Post freelancer job on Upwork/Fiverr (e.g., "South African 2D explainer video animator")
3. **Week 2-3**: Review proposals, select animator
4. **Week 3-7**: Animation production (animator works while you coordinate)
5. **Week 7-8**: Revisions, captions, platform optimization
6. **End of Week 8**: Ready for deployment

---

## FAST-TRACKING STRATEGIES

1. **Use animation templates** (Canva, Animaker) - saves 30-40% production time
2. **Simple character designs** - minimalist flat design reduces complexity
3. **Shorter scenes** - cut each scene to essentials (currently 8 scenes = ~3-4 min video, could reduce to 2-2.5 min)
4. **Royalty-free music** - skip custom composition
5. **Outsource voiceover only** - use AI voiceover (e.g., Google TTS, Eleven Labs) to save recording time
6. **Parallel production** - start animating Scene 1 while voiceover is being recorded
