# Production Brief & Animator Guidelines

## PROJECT OVERVIEW

**Project Name**: Municipal Water Fault Reporting - Public Awareness Video
**Client**: South African Municipality
**Purpose**: Educate residents on proper water fault reporting procedures without creating duplicate complaints
**Target Audience**: All South African residents aged 16+
**Deliverable**: Professional 2D animated explainer video
**Final Duration**: 3-4 minutes
**Delivery Formats**: 16:9 (YouTube/Web), 9:16 (Instagram/TikTok vertical), 1:1 (Instagram Feed)

---

## PROJECT GOALS

1. **Reduce duplicate complaints** by explaining the importance of reference numbers
2. **Clarify the 48-hour period** - attendance time, NOT resolution time
3. **Empower residents** to follow up confidently using original reference numbers
4. **Create professional, shareable content** suitable for WhatsApp distribution
5. **Reach diverse audiences** across age groups and education levels
6. **Establish clear municipal communication** about fault reporting procedures

---

## CREATIVE DIRECTION

### Visual Style
- **Animation Type**: Clean 2D flat design with subtle depth
- **Aesthetic**: Modern, professional, corporate but approachable
- **Complexity**: Simple shapes, bold outlines, minimal details
- **Movement**: Smooth transitions, 24fps standard animation
- **Setting**: Contemporary South African suburban/urban residential environments

### Tone & Voice
- **Overall Tone**: Friendly but serious, empowering but cautionary
- **Voiceover Style**: Professional South African English, warm and clear
- **Music Mood**: Uplifting, modern, instrumental (corporate explainer genre)
- **Pace**: Deliberate and clear, not rushed

### Character Design Philosophy
- **Diversity**: Include 4-5 residents representing various ages, genders, ethnicities, and backgrounds
- **Relatability**: Modern clothing, smartphones, realistic home settings
- **Expressions**: Clear, legible facial expressions showing emotional progression
- **Consistency**: Same character(s) used across multiple scenes where relevant

### Color Psychology
- **Green (#2ECC71)**: Positive actions, correct procedures, resolution
- **Red (#E63946)**: Warnings, mistakes, what NOT to do
- **Blue (#3498DB)**: Information, municipality, authority
- **Gray (#95A5A6)**: Neutral, waiting periods, background elements
- **Yellow/Gold (#F39C12)**: Attention, highlighting important elements (reference numbers)

## ANIMATION SPECIFICATIONS

### Frame Rate & Timing
- **Frame Rate**: 24 fps (standard for smooth web video)
- **Scene Duration**: As specified in script (20-35 seconds each)
- **Transitions**: 0.5-1 second fades or slides between scenes
- **Text Timing**: 3-5 seconds minimum per text element

### Animation Style
- **Keyframe Animation**: Smooth easing (ease-in-out for natural motion)
- **Character Movement**: 2-3 frames per second walk cycles (efficient but smooth)
- **Object Animation**: Appear/disappear with fade or slide transitions
- **Text Animation**: Appear word-by-word or line-by-line for emphasis
- **Effects**: Subtle parallax, shadows, and depth where needed

### Complexity Guidelines
- **Priority 1**: Character facial expressions and emotions (MUST be clear)
- **Priority 2**: Text readability and timing (MUST match voiceover)
- **Priority 3**: Reference number clarity (MUST be prominent)
- **Priority 4**: Background details (can be simplified if needed)

**Simplification Strategy**: If timeline/budget tight, prioritize character animation and text over background details.

## GRAPHIC ELEMENTS & ICONS

### Required Graphics
1. **Phone Screen UI**
   - Call interface
   - Reference number display
   - Contact screen
   - SMS/message interface
   - Simple, modern design

2. **Reference Number Display**
   - Large, bold text in yellow/gold box
   - Format: "REFERENCE: 123456789"
   - Multiple variations for different contexts
   - Always high contrast and readable

3. **Timeline/Flowchart Elements**
   - Vertical and horizontal arrow connectors
   - Circular milestone markers
   - Branching pathways (for duplicate complaints)
   - Checkmark indicators (for correct procedures)

4. **Icons & Symbols**
   - Warning triangle (⚠️ - for mistakes)
   - Checkmark (✓ - for correct actions)
   - Clock (for time passing)
   - Calendar (for days passing)
   - Phone (for communication)
   - Water droplet/pipe (for water fault)

5. **Text Boxes & Labels**
   - Thought bubbles (for resident's confusion/thoughts)
   - Speech bubbles (for resident speaking)
   - Highlight boxes (for important info)
   - Information banners

### Design Requirements for Graphics
- **Consistency**: All graphics should match overall style (flat, bold outlines)
- **Readability**: All icons must be clear at 1080p resolution
- **Color Coding**: Use color scheme (green=good, red=bad, blue=info, yellow=attention)
- **Scalability**: All graphics should be vector-based for quality at any size

### Voiceover Talent Requirements
- **Accent**: South African English (preferred), clear and professional
- **Gender**: Open (male or female acceptable)
- **Age**: Mature/adult voice (30-50+ ideal)
- **Tone**: Warm, authoritative but not condescending, clear diction
- **Pace**: Moderate, allowing time for on-screen text to be read

### Voiceover Recording Details
- **Sample Rate**: 48kHz (for video synchronization)
- **Bit Depth**: 24-bit
- **Format**: WAV (lossless)
- **Delivery**: One master file with all voiceover lines

### Voiceover Timing
- Total voiceover length: Approximately 2:30-3:00 minutes
- Each scene has specific voiceover duration (see Scene-by-Scene Script)
- Timing must be locked to animation (animation follows voiceover timing)

## MUSIC & SOUND DESIGN

### Background Music
- **Genre**: Corporate/Explainer (modern, uplifting, instrumental)
- **Tempo**: 90-120 BPM (moderate, steady pace)
- **Mood**: Professional, confident, slightly motivational
- **Duration**: Full 3-4 minute track
- **Instrumentation**: Modern synths, soft strings, light percussion
- **Licensing**: Royalty-free or licensed for commercial use
- **Volume Level**: -18dB mix (background, not primary)

### Sound Effects
- **Scene 1**: Water splash/leak sound (1-2 seconds, -12dB)
- **Scene 2**: Clock ticking in background (subtle, -15dB)
- **Scene 3**: Notification "ping" sound for each new complaint (3x, -12dB each)
- **Scene 3**: Optional whoosh/alert sound with warning symbol
- **Scene 6**: Phone call connecting sound (1 second, -12dB)
- **Overall**: Keep effects subtle and non-distracting

### Audio Levels
- Voiceover: -3dB (primary, always audible)
- Background Music: -18dB (supporting)
- Sound Effects: -12dB (noticeable but not overwhelming)
- Master Mix: -3dB to -6dB (leaves headroom, prevents clipping)

### Audio Format & Delivery
- Final Mix: Stereo, 48kHz, 24-bit, exported as WAV
- Separate Tracks: Voiceover (isolated), Music (isolated), Effects (isolated)
- Captions: Burned-in SRT file for all platforms

## COMMON PITFALLS TO AVOID

1. **Making the 48-hour message ambiguous** - Be explicit: "attendance, NOT completion"
2. **Overcrowding scenes with details** - Keep it simple and focused
3. **Using reference numbers inconsistently** - Same format throughout (e.g., always "REFERENCE: 123456789")
4. **Making text too small** - Test readability on iPhone 6S (smallest common device)
5. **Fast/unclear character emotions** - Hold emotional expressions for 2+ seconds
6. **Inconsistent South African representation** - Ensure settings and characters feel authentically local
7. **Poor audio sync** - Voiceover timing is CRITICAL - lock it in early
8. **Over-animating** - Restrain yourself; simple movements often work better
9. **Ignoring mobile viewing** - Always test on actual phones/small screens
10. **Losing the core message** - Every element should serve the educational purpose
